# Template: Selected Work (anonymized case study)

Reuses the exact structure already proven in `HireHax_Deck.pdf`'s "Selected Work" section. Don't invent a new case-study format when this one is already written, approved, and on-brand. Post roughly once a month, in whichever weekly slot fits.

## Structure (from the deck, verbatim pattern)

```
[STAGE / SECTOR TAG, small caps or bold — e.g. "SERIES C · EUROPEAN DIAGNOSTICS"]

[One-line summary of what was done, stated as an outcome-oriented headline.]

[Two to three sentences: the situation, what HireHax actually did — be specific about the mechanism (mapped the org, designed the architecture, placed the role, restructured the cadence), not just "we helped."]

OUTCOME: [One or two sentences, with a real number, timeframe, or concrete state change. This is the line that carries the whole post's credibility.]
```

## Rules specific to this format

- Anonymize the company. Stage + sector + one distinguishing detail (e.g. "PE-backed life sciences platform") is enough specificity without naming names, consistent with the deck's own approach and the SOW's confidentiality commitments.
- The outcome line must contain a number or a concrete, checkable state ("CCO still in role," "cycle time reduced by 41 percent"). A case study without a hard outcome is just an anecdote and undermines the pillar's purpose.
- Never fabricate or round up a number. If the real number isn't strong enough to state precisely, don't publish that case study yet.
