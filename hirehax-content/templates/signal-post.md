# Template: Signal post (weekly, short)

**Pillar 5.1 — Signal.** Purpose: prove HireHax is intelligence-led, not reactive. Keep it short — this is the pillar readers scan, not study.

## Structure

1. **The fact.** One piece of market movement: a funding round, a leadership exit/appointment, a clinical milestone, an M&A move. Named company, named number or stage, no editorializing yet.
2. **The read.** One or two sentences on what it means for leadership, not for the deal itself. This is the part a generic news account can't write.
3. **The implication, stated plainly.** What should a CEO or board reading this be asking themselves right now. No call to action, no "DM me" — the implication itself is the value.

## Length

60-120 words. If it's running longer, it's actually a Search/Advisory pillar post wearing a Signal costume — split it.

## Skeleton

```
[Company] raised [$ / stage] this week[, or: named a new [role] in [name]].

[One sentence on what this signals about the market or the leadership bar at this stage.]

[One plain sentence naming the question this raises for boards/CEOs at a similar stage.]
```

## Worked example

```
A European diagnostics company closed its Series C this week at a valuation that assumes commercial execution most incumbent leadership teams haven't had to deliver before.

Capital moved faster than the org chart did. That gap is where Series C boards usually discover their commercial leader was hired for Series B.

The question worth asking before the next board meeting: does the executive team you have match the company you just became, or the company you were six months ago.
```
