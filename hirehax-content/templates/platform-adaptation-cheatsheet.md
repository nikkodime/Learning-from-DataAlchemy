# Platform adaptation cheatsheet

Rules from the knowledge base, Section 1 "Platform adaptation," made actionable. The rule that matters most: **adapt the same central argument to each platform, don't copy one caption everywhere.** Per the knowledge base's own generation instructions: "Adapt the same central argument to each requested platform rather than copying one caption everywhere."

| Platform | Role | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Primary, strongest strategic depth | Longest caption, full executive framing, one clear lesson stated explicitly. This is where the argument gets its most complete treatment. |
| **Instagram / Reels** | Emotional hook, discovery | Tight emotional hook, clean short paragraphs, punchline, restrained hashtags (2-4). |
| **TikTok** | Conversational reach | Fast, provocative, simple caption. Least formal register of the set, but never generic or off-voice. |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Searchable discovery | Searchable title (what the audience would type), concise description. |
| **Facebook** | Accessible professional | Accessible professional framing, plus a discussion prompt to invite comments. |
| **Threads / X** | Compression | Compress to the single central contrast or truth. One or two lines. Not a re-post of the LinkedIn caption. |

## The adaptation test

For each platform version, ask: could someone who only saw this platform's version state the piece's central truth back accurately? If not, it's been compressed past the point of making sense, and needs a rewrite rather than a further trim.

## Sequencing

Per `00-strategy.md` §7: film and write the spoken script first, then generate all platform versions the same session using `templates/video-script-master.md`. Publish same-day or next-day across platforms rather than staggering by more than 24-48 hours — the knowledge base's formula is built around one strong hook doing work across the whole distribution, and that effect weakens if the platforms go out days apart.
