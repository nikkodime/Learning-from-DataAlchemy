# Template: Monthly newsletter

Per the July 9 internal decision: monthly cadence, published on the website first, distributed via LinkedIn and email. This is the long-form home for the Signal pillar plus one expanded piece.

## Structure

1. **Subject line.** Plain, specific, no hype. Pattern: "[Month] leadership signal: [the single most important item]." Not "5 things you need to know."
2. **Opening note.** Two to three sentences, Nikolay's own read on the month, one point of view stated directly. Not a table of contents.
3. **Signal digest.** Three to six items from that month's LinkedIn Signal posts, tightened, with the "implication" line kept for each. This is a compilation, not new writing.
4. **Expanded piece.** One thought-leadership or principle post from that month, expanded to 400-600 words. Give it room the LinkedIn version didn't have.
5. **Selected Work** (if one was published that month, or held in reserve for the newsletter specifically).
6. **Close.** One line, direct, no salesy CTA. Pattern: "If your leadership team doesn't match the company you're building toward, that conversation is worth having early. [email]."

## Rules

- Total length: 600-900 words. Long enough to be worth a subscription, short enough to read in one sitting.
- Every voice rule from `01-voice-and-style.md` applies at newsletter length too, including the dash rule and the ban on exclamation marks and emoji.
- Never pad with generic industry news. If a month is light on signal, run a shorter issue. Consistency of cadence matters more than length.

## Skeleton

```
SUBJECT: [Month] leadership signal: [headline item]

[Opening note: 2-3 sentences, one clear point of view on the month.]

THIS MONTH'S SIGNAL

- [Item 1: fact + implication, tightened from the LinkedIn post]
- [Item 2: fact + implication]
- [Item 3: fact + implication]

[EXPANDED PIECE TITLE]

[400-600 words, expanded from that month's strongest thought-leadership or principle post.]

[Selected Work, if applicable — full case-study block from templates/case-study.md]

[Close line + contact.]
```
