This is the piece the prior knowledge base didn't have: a single tracker of what's already been developed (don't repeat it by accident) versus what's queued and ready to script (`AVAILABLE`) versus what's been used since this system started (`SHOT`). Update the **Status** column as items move through `05-production-workflow.md`. Full text for anything marked `DEVELOPED` lives in `source/HireHax_Social_Content_Claude_Knowledge_Base.md` Section 2.

## Legend

- `DEVELOPED` — full or recovered script already exists in the knowledge base. Do not re-script without a deliberate sequel/new-angle decision.
- `AVAILABLE` — topic-bank idea or hook, approved but not yet scripted. Pull from here for new content.
- `SHOT` — scripted and published since this system started. Move items here as they go out; add the publish date.

## How to use this file

1. Before scripting anything new, check here first — pick the next `AVAILABLE` item in whichever pillar is due per the calendar (`03-content-calendar.md`), or propose a deliberate sequel to a `DEVELOPED` item with a materially different angle (per the KB's own rule 3 in Section 6).
2. When a script is finished and published, move it from `AVAILABLE` to a dated `SHOT` line at the top of its pillar table.
3. When the `AVAILABLE` pool in a pillar runs low (under 3 items), that's the trigger to brainstorm new topic-bank ideas for that pillar specifically, not to start repeating old ones.
